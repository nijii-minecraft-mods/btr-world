# BTR World

A **Bocchi the Rock! fan mod** for Minecraft Java **1.21.1**, built with **Fabric and Polymer**. Tame a tiny band, decorate a rehearsal room and let your companions play at their instruments.

## Companions

Bocchi, Nijika, Ryo and Kita come in both tsum-style plush and chibi forms: **eight companion types**.

- Feed their favourite food to tame them. Use an empty hand to make them wait or follow.
- Choose normal following, close following or wandering around the owner.
- Breed companions of different heroines and forms. The child inherits a heroine identity, not a blend of visual traits.
- Feed babies to shorten the remaining growth time.
- Bocchi flees danger and hides; Ryo eats grass and trades figurines; Kita sparkles and provides night vision; Nijika offers nearby haste and helps calm Bocchi; Seika protects with resistance; Kikuri grants strength and drinks honey; PA-san grants night vision and slows hostile mobs.

## Rehearsal room

There are **85 blocks and 113 crafting recipes**, including 63 colour-palette blocks, figurines, drums, microphones, a guitar stand, amplifiers, a cardboard box you can sit in, plush toys, posters, a neon star and fairy lights. Costumes and accessories are decorative.

Adult tame companions can approach their matching instrument and perform for **90–150 seconds**, with note particles and original note-block phrases. Nijika plays percussion, Kita flute tones, Bocchi guitar, Ryo and Kikuri bass, Seika livehouse rhythm, and PA-san synth beats. No anime soundtrack recordings, lyrics or song transcriptions are included. Models currently have no animated hands.

Seven chibi statue structures can generate in the Overworld. Ten advancements have Russian, Belarusian and English translations.

## Installation

Requires **Java 21**, **Fabric Loader**, **Fabric API** and **Polymer bundled**, all for Minecraft 1.21.1.

Install the mod and dependencies on the server and configure Polymer's server resource-pack hosting. **Joining Java clients do not need BTR World, but must accept the server resource pack.** Install the same dependencies in a local profile for singleplayer. For JEI integration, install BTR World and its dependencies on the client together with Fabric JEI. Bedrock/Geyser is not verified.

## Quick controls

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Tame / heal / accelerate growth | Favourite food: Bocchi cookies, Nijika cake item, Ryo wheat, Kita glow berries, Seika sweet berries, Kikuri honey bottle, PA-san dried kelp |
| Wait / resume | Empty-hand right-click near the base of your companion |
| Movement mode | Sneak + right-click with a stick |
| Music on/off | Sneak + empty-hand right-click |
| Sit inside the placed cardboard box | Right-click; sneak to exit |

Custom items are in the **BTR World** tab in `/polymer creative`. Operators can locate statues with `/locate structure bocchi:heroine_statue`.

## Beta and disclosures

This version is a beta. Automated build, resource and inheritance checks do not replace gameplay testing. Use a test world before adding it to a long-running server.

A substantial amount of the code, assets, translations and documentation was produced with generative AI assistance, based on Aliska's ideas and feedback. The mod does not use generative AI at runtime. This project is prepared for unlisted distribution under Modrinth's current policies, subject to moderation.

Unofficial fan project; not affiliated with Bocchi the Rock!'s creators, Mojang or Microsoft. MIT covers the project's own implementation and does not grant rights to the original franchise. No original anime frames, audio recordings or third-party model files are bundled.

## Concert venues

New chunks can contain STARRY-inspired, school festival and indie-club buildings. Each has a full set of equipment and four living Chibi/Tsum companions. Untamed stage residents perform original note-block phrases for nearby spectators and can be befriended. Companion names use consistent Russian, Belarusian and English translations.
